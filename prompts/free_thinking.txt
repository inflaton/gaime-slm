### Entity Identification and Classification Rules

1. General Approach:
   - Extract entities without assumptions or constraints.
   - Identify all possible segments and interpret them contextually.
   - Consider multiple interpretations for ambiguous segments.

2. Entity Classification Rules:
    - INTERMEDIARY: Payment facilitators or intermediaries that process payments on behalf of merchants (e.g., SYNTHETIC PAY A, SYNTHETIC PAY B, SYNTHETIC PAY C). Often appears before MERCHANT_NAME.
        - Example: "SYN* SYNTHETIC MARKET #0001" → {"INTERMEDIARY": "SYN*", "MERCHANT_NAME": "SYNTHETIC MARKET #0001"}
    - MERCHANT_NAME: Represents the primary business where the transaction occurred.
        - Merchants with large number of retail locations may include store numbers, Examples: "SYNTHETIC MARKET#0001", "SYNTHETIC CAFE 00002", "SYNTHETIC MART 0000000003", "SYNTHETIC FUEL 00000000004".
        - If a brand name is immediately followed by product or service text, include the entire portion (Example: "SYNTHETIC SOFTWARE * CLOUD STORAGE" → {"MERCHANT_NAME": "SYNTHETIC SOFTWARE * CLOUD STORAGE"})
    - DYNAMIC_DESCRIPTOR: Refers to transaction-specific identifiers — like order numbers, reference codes, or invoice details—that often appear in eCommerce (online) transactions and provide clarity on individual purchases.
        - Examples: "*INV-0001", "-DEMO00002", "* O #00003", "DEMO04", "* DEMO00005", "*DEMO00006"
    - STREET: Street address.
        - Typically starts with a number or building identifier, followed by street name and known street suffixes (e.g., ST, AVE, BLVD).
        - Example: "123 Synthetic AvenueSte 100"
    - ZIP: 5- or 9-digit postal codes.
    - CITY: Recognizable US city or town names.
    - STATE: Commonly a two-letter US state abbreviation (e.g., "VA" for Virginia).
    - COUNTRY: Two- or three-letter country codes (e.g., "USA", "US").
    - PHONE: Phone numbers in any format (e.g., "202-555-0100", "2025550101", "+12025550102", "(202)555-0103").
    - WEBSITE: Website/URL associated with the merchant (e.g., "MERCHANT.EXAMPLE/BILL", "PAY.EXAMPLE/HELP#", "HTTPSWWW.DELIVERY.EXAMPLE").

3. Handling Special Cases:
    - If a token might fit multiple categories, consider its position or formatting to determine the correct entity type.
    - If you are unsure about how to label a token, use your best guess.
    - If duplicate or partially duplicate information (e.g., website, street, city, state, or country) appears at the end of MERCHANT_NAME, remove it from MERCHANT_NAME and assign it to the appropriate entity key.
    - Omit complete and partial duplicate strings
